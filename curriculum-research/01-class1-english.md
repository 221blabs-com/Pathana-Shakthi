## What search summaries say about a Class 1 English book (older edition, 2019-20)

These come from a third-party scan of the **2019-20** textbook *My English World*
(cdn1.byjus.com) and a teacher-site table. It is very likely **not** the 2024-25 FLN book, which
may have been rewritten for Tholimettu. Use only as background.

- 8 units, "each unit dealing with a specific theme but all the units together making a story";
  main characters Ammu and her pet squirrel Bittu. [S-secondary]
- Unit list from one teacher-site table, not confirmed by a second source:

| # | Unit | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ammu and her family | Family |
| 2 | Going to school | Travel and community services |
| 3 | Ammu's school | School |
| 4 | Ammu and her friends at her school | Friendship |
| 5 | Things I do | Good habits |
| 6 | At the zoo | Environment |
| 7 | Fruits and vegetables | Health |
| 8 | Story time: Princess Tomato's Marriage | Entertainment |

- Unit 1 opens with a story about Ammu, has a "Sing the song" section about her family, a
  "Reading my name" rhyme pairing letters with words, and a colouring activity. [S-secondary]

## Class 1 English expectations from other documents

- Period steps for English under Tholimettu (see 02): face-sheet activities → listening text →
  reading text (picture interaction, individual, collaborative, model reading) → post-reading →
  editing. [S-secondary]
- Class 1 English focus (alignment sheets): oral language development, picture interaction,
  alphabet readiness. [S-secondary]
- Worksheets have Level 1 "Readiness" (based on the preceding class) and Level 2 (current
  class). [S-secondary]
- Class 1 ORF target 20 wpm (Tholimettu), writing simple words. [S-secondary]
- NCERT Class I English outcomes quoted: "name common objects like man, dog etc. when pictures
  are shown"; "Recites poems and rhymes along with actions". [S-official, summary]
