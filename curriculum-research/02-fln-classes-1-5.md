# 02 — FLN, Classes 1-5 (Telugu, English, Maths, EVS)

Status: partial; programme structure only. No textbook, handbook or period plan was opened.

## Programme: Tholimettu / First Step [S-secondary]

- Classes 1-5, all government schools; started 2022-23 with Central Square Foundation; running in
  2025-26. Start dates conflict between sources (12 June vs 15 August rollout).
- SCERT materials per subject (Telugu, English, Maths, EVS; Telugu and English medium):
  teacher handbooks, **year plan** (month-wise, week-wise, day-wise), **unit/lesson plans**,
  **daily period plans**, FLN workbooks for Classes 1-5, remediation modules (a Telugu module for
  Classes 3-5 is reported to have 56 daily sessions — single 2026 blog, unverified).
- Unit length about 10-15 periods; bridge activities about 5 periods.

## Steps to be followed in a period (Intensive Implementation Fortnight orders) [S-secondary]

Quoted in the summary of the paatashaala page:

- **English**: Facesheet based activities – Listening text and related activities – Reading text
  – Picture interaction – Individual Reading – Collaborative Reading – Model Reading – Post Reading
  Activities – Editing.
- **Telugu / Urdu**: Oral Language Development – Introduction of keywords – Reading Activities
  (model / shared / individual reading) – Comprehension Activities – Practice/Writing – Story time.
