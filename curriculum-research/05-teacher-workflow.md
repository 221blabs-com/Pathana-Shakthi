# 05 — A Telangana primary teacher's year, week and day

Status: partial. The teacher handbooks, academic calendar and register formats were not read.

## Year [S-secondary unless noted]

| When (2025-26) | What | App support idea |
|---|---|---|
| June (school reopens) | Readiness / bridge activities (worksheets Level 1, about 5 periods each) | readiness pack per class |
| 25-30 June | FLN **baseline** test; results to the Telangana School Education App by 15 July | baseline recording + export |
| through the year | Follow SCERT **year plan** (month/week/day), unit plans (10-15 periods each), daily period plans | Today view from the year plan |
| FA1 … FA4 (dates per yearly calendar; one site gives FA1 5 Oct 2026) | formative assessment with four tools | FA tool record per child |
| 25-30 Nov | FLN **midline**; results by 15 Dec | midline recording |
| SA1 (one site: 1-8 Dec 2026), SA2 (end of year) | summative | SA marks, grade bands |
| 4-7 March (endline reported for LIP) | **endline** | endline + year report |

## Week / day

- Daily: teach the period plan; the subject's period steps (English: face sheet → listening → reading
  in four modes → post-reading → editing; Telugu: oral language → keywords → reading → comprehension →
  writing → story time).
- Remediation sessions where a module exists (Telugu 3-5).
- Slip tests unannounced within a normal period (~40 min).

## Registers and records

- High schools: SCERT **CCE register for FA and SA** (official) — FA average 20% + SA2 80%.

  Vidyalaya circular was found, which is not Telangana).
