## CCE structure

- Four formative assessments (FA1-FA4) and two summatives (SA1, SA2) per year. [S-secondary]
- **Weightage (official register)**: the SCERT "CCE Register for FA and SA (HS)" says the
  annual grade is "calculated based on the average of four Formative Assessments (20%), and the
  marks achieved by the students in second Summative Assessment (80%)", with no SA2 recorded
  for Class X (board exam instead). [S-official, summary]. A generic CCE page claiming 10%
  per FA and 30% per SA is **not** Telangana's.
- **FA tools** (four) [S-secondary]: (1) observation / children's participation and reflection
  in class; (2) written works ("own expressions in notebooks, field observation, data collection
  and analysis reports, creative writing"); (3) project works ("preparation and presentation of
  projects, model making, art, paintings"); (4) slip test ("informal … no prior notice … in a
  regular period of about 40 minutes").
- **FA marks**: Classes 6-10, 5 marks per tool (20 per FA). Classes 1-5, sources conflict: 10/10/10/20
  (total 50) or 10/10/20. [S-secondary]
- **Grades**: one FA table: 91-100% A+, 71-90% A, 51-70% B+, 41-50% B, 0-40% C (primary).
  A high-school table on 50 marks: A1 46-50 … D2 0-17. [S-secondary; verify]
